Layout

Full width, page padding px-5 sm:px-8 lg:px-10; rows fill; nothing scrolls sideways from 360 px up.

Do · full width, rows fill

Cards grow to share their row: flex flex-wrap, children flex-[1_1_18rem].

Don't · a narrow column

Empty sides waste the screen.

Don't · a gap at the end

A half-empty last row looks unfinished.

Spacing

Tailwind's 4 px scale only. Related things close (8–12 px), groups further apart (24–48 px).

Accessibility

Real elements, a label for every field, keyboard everywhere, 24 px targets, alt on images, motion off under reduced motion.

Do · a real label

A <label>, not a placeholder that disappears while typing.

Do · visible focus

Tab through the page: the ring always shows where you are.

Modern practice

The platform before JavaScript, matched to how widely each feature works (Baseline, Nov 2026); undo over "Are you sure?"; check a field when it's left.

How widely it works
Use freely

<dialog>, inert, :has(), container queries, dvh, safe areas, :user-invalid, color-scheme

With a fallback

popover, @starting-style, text-wrap: balance, field-sizing, view transitions, anchor positioning

Only as an extra

cross-page view transitions, customizable <select>, text-wrap: pretty

The screen must work without anything in the last group.

Before you ship

Words too: real content, never lorem ipsum; actions are verbs; errors say what to do.

  • Full width at 390, 768, 1280 and 1920 px; no gaps, no sideways scroll
  • The theme's tokens and fonts only; right in light and dark
  • Loading, empty and error states
  • Keyboard works; labels, alt and aria-label in place; text 4.5:1
  • Looked at it rendered, not just in code
