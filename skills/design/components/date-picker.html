Date picker

Typed fields for a date people know; a calendar in a popover when seeing the month helps (a booking, a range); the browser's <input type="date"> for a plain single date, on phones too.

Impossible dates disabled

Typed fields

A fieldset asking the question, an example hint, Day / Month / Year labeled; numeric keyboard, bday-* autocomplete; never auto-jump between fields.

An error

Date issued must be a real date

Only the wrong field is marked; the whole date when it's empty or out of range.

Messages
  • Enter your date of birth

Say what to do, naming the date. Month accepts "3", "mar" or "march".

Calendar in a popover

An outline trigger with the date or "Pick a date"; month names spelled out, today marked, impossible dates disabled; picking closes it.

Single date

Today (the 4th) is tinted, earlier days disabled. Pick a day: it fills the button and closes.

Do · typing still works

A text field plus a "Choose date" button: people who know the date just type it.

Ranges

Two months side by side; start and end marked, the days between highlighted; an end before the start can't be picked.

Range

Click a start, then an end. While choosing the end, earlier days are disabled; the months don't move.

Before you ship

In code: native <input type="date" min max> (Baseline; value always yyyy-mm-dd, one date, not a range). Calendar: the WAI-ARIA date picker dialog (input + "Choose date" button; arrows by day and week, Page Up/Down by month, Enter picks, Esc closes and returns focus; aria-selected on the chosen day). shadcn: Popover + Calendar, mode="single|range", numberOfMonths={2}, timeZone.

  • Known dates typed in Day / Month / Year, with the right autocomplete
  • Calendars only for dates near today or ranges; typing still works
  • Today marked; impossible dates disabled
  • Ranges: two months, start and end clear, the end can't precede the start
  • Keyboard: arrows in the grid, Esc closes and returns focus
