Labels and layout

Every control has its own clickable label in positive words. A switch's label stays the same in both states.

Do

Positive and specific: checked means yes.

Don't

A negative: people have to work out what checking it does.

Before you ship

In code: native inputs with Basecoat, <label class="label"><input type="checkbox" class="input">…</label>; radios share a name; a switch is <input type="checkbox" role="switch" class="input">; groups in <fieldset class="fieldset"> with a <legend>. This theme's --input is very light: add not-checked:border-muted-foreground. Don't rely on accent-color (not Baseline); Basecoat draws the boxes. Keyboard (APG): Space toggles; in a radio group, arrows move and check.

  • Radios for one, checkboxes for many, switches only where changes apply at once
  • Every control has a clickable label in positive words
  • Groups in a fieldset with a question as legend
  • Questions have no pre-selected radio; settings show their current value
  • Unchecked controls are clearly visible in light and dark
