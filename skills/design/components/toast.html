Toast

A toast is a short notice that floats over the page after an action or from the system, and goes away by itself. Anything critical, or anything people must fix, is an alert on the page or a dialog.

Short, never the only place

One line: what happened.

Undo, not "Are you sure?"

For everyday actions.

With an action, it stays

Until used or closed.

One corner, safe areas

Clear of notches; announced politely.

Undo instead of asking

Do everyday actions at once and offer Undo. Ask first only when it's serious and can't be undone; asking every time teaches people to click Yes without reading.

Do · archive one, then Undo
  • Invoice #0412 from Ember & Oak
  • Team lunch on Friday
  • Your report is ready

Done at once; the toast with Undo stays until used or closed. The empty toaster was in the page from the start.

Don't · for an everyday action
Are you sure?

Do you really want to archive this conversation?

A question every time gets clicked through, and "Yes" names nothing.

Do · serious and final
Delete "Ember & Oak site"?

Its 14 files are deleted too. This can't be undone.

Can't be undone: ask, naming the thing and the outcome.

A few words, the most important point, no full stop. One action at most, 1–2 words.

Timing and place

Without an action it may go after a few seconds, if it's said elsewhere too. With an action, no timer. One corner, clear of the safe areas.

Before you ship

In code: React, shadcn/ui's Toast or Sonner (toast.success(), toast.promise()). Basecoat: an empty <div class="toaster" aria-live="polite"> in the page from the start; each .toast (role="status", data-category) holds a .toast-content with an icon, <section> and an optional <footer> action. A toast never takes focus.

  • Nothing critical or only-said-here is in a toast
  • Everyday deletes and archives happen at once, with Undo in the toast
  • A toast with an action doesn't time out
  • One corner, clear of the safe areas
  • The toaster is in the page from load; focus never moves to a toast
