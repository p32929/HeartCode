Badge

A badge is a small label on an item: its status or a count. It only shows; an action is a button, going somewhere is a link.

Not clickable

No link or button dressed as a badge.

One or two words

A state: "Active", not "Activate".

Quiet by default

Secondary or outline; solid sparingly.

One meaning per color

The word says it too. Red only for errors.

Before you ship

In code: <span class="badge"> (Basecoat), plus data-variant="secondary|outline|destructive|ghost"; a Lucide icon goes inside.

  • No badge is a link or a button
  • Every badge is one or two words, a state, sentence case
  • Mostly secondary or outline; solid and red only where they mean something
  • The same status looks the same everywhere
  • Counts sit next to what they count
