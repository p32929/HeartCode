Sidebar

A sidebar is an app's main navigation, a vertical list of sections on the left: for many or growing sections and apps used every day. A top nav suits a handful of sections and leaves the content more room.

Sidebar or top nav

Many or growing sections: sidebar. A few: top nav. Never both.

A visible word on every item

Left-aligned; the icon only beside it.

Current page stands out

By more than color, with aria-current.

Visible on desktop

On phones, a sheet from a menu button.

Before you ship

In code: <nav aria-label> with <a href> links in lists. shadcn: SidebarProvider › Sidebar (SidebarHeader, SidebarContent › SidebarGroup, SidebarFooter), SidebarMenuButton isActive, SidebarTrigger. Basecoat: <aside class="sidebar"> with sidebar.min.js.

  • Sidebar or top nav chosen by the number of sections; links not repeated in both
  • Every item has a visible, left-aligned label
  • Current page: filled and bolder, with aria-current="page"
  • Phones: a menu button opens it as a modal sheet (page inert, Esc closes); a link closes it; height in dvh
  • Collapsed icons have tooltips and accessible names
