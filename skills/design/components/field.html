Which one

Input for a short answer, Textarea for longer text; every one with a label, in a Field.

Input

A short, one-line answer: name, email, amount.

Textarea

Longer than one line. field-sizing-content grows it as they type (try it), max-h-40 caps it; rows is the fallback height.

Fieldset
Address

Related fields under one legend.

Label, help and placeholder

Label above, short, sentence case, no colon. Help: one short muted sentence under the control. Set type, autocomplete, inputmode and enterkeyhint.

Do

This appears on invoices and emails.

A visible label; the help stays in view while they type.

Don't

Placeholder as the label or hint: gone once they type, easy to mistake for a filled-in value.

Width follows the content

A short field hints a short answer; not every field full width.

Required and optional

One convention per form, and required on the control either way.

Mark the optional ones

Most fields required? Add "(optional)" to the few that aren't.

Or mark every required one

An asterisk on every required field, not just some.

Errors

Checked when the person leaves the field, not while they type (live only for rules like a new password's). Right under the control, red, saying what to do. Keep what they typed.

Checked on leaving

Type "sam@", then Tab away: red only after it's been used (user-invalid:), with aria-invalid and the message.

Do

Enter an email address like name@example.com

Says how to fix it; the entry stays to correct.

Don't

Invalid input. This field is required.

"Invalid", "required": nothing about what to do, and the entry wiped.

Before you ship

In code: <label for> matched to the control's id; Basecoat <div role="group" class="field"> with the label, control and a <p> for help; invalid: data-invalid on the field, aria-invalid="true" and aria-describedby on the control, the error in <p role="alert">; groups in <fieldset class="fieldset"> with a <legend>.

  • Every control has a visible <label>; no placeholder used as one
  • Textarea only for multi-line answers; widths fit the answers
  • Required/optional marked one way across the form
  • Checked when the person leaves a field; errors under it say how to fix it, with aria-invalid and aria-describedby
  • type, autocomplete, inputmode and enterkeyhint set
