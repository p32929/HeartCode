Sheet

A sheet slides in from an edge for a secondary task done without leaving the page. A drawer is the bottom sheet for phones, dragged by its handle. A short task belongs in a dialog, a long flow on its own page.

Brief and secondary

Never page-to-page navigation or long flows.

One at a time

Never a sheet on top of a sheet.

A title and a visible ✕

Swiping is never the only way out.

The footer stays put

Cancel beside Save; only the body scrolls.

Which one

Same task on desktop and phone? A dialog on desktop, a drawer on a phone. A sheet blocks the page: if people need what it covers, use a page.

Dialog

A short task or a decision, centered. See dialog.md.

Sheet

A secondary task on a larger screen; the page stays in place behind.

Drawer

On a phone: a brief task or a few options.

Page

Long or complex flows, anything people stay in.

Before you ship

In code: a native <dialog> opened with showModal() (the page behind inert, focus inside, Esc closes, ::backdrop dims the page; a sheet built another way sets inert on the page), aria-labelledby → the title. Basecoat: <dialog class="drawer" data-side="right"> (left, top; none = bottom) › <article> › header (h2, p), section (scrolls), footer, plus a ghost ✕ button. Safe area: pb-[calc(1rem+env(safe-area-inset-bottom))] with viewport-fit=cover.

  • A brief secondary task; never a sheet on a sheet, never for page-to-page navigation
  • A title and a visible ✕; Esc, click outside and (phones) Back close it
  • Footer visible while the body scrolls; Cancel beside the primary
  • Bottom sheets: handle, at most 100dvh minus 6rem, footer clear of the home bar
