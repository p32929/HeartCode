Alert

An alert is a message inside the page, near what it's about, and it stays until fixed or dismissed. A notice that comes and goes is a toast; a critical one that must be answered now is a dialog.

Alert, toast or dialog

By how much it must interrupt.

Near what it's about

Top of the content; in a form, above its buttons.

The point, then what to do

One or two short sentences.

Stays until fixed

Never on a timer. Few per page.

Words · placement

The title says what happened, the text how to fix it. In a form, just above the buttons. Shown later: role="alert" if urgent, role="status" otherwise.

Before you ship

In code: <div class="alert"> (data-variant="destructive") with a Lucide icon, a heading, a <section> and an optional <footer> action. Shown later: role="alert" for errors and urgent things (interrupts), role="status" for the rest (polite; the empty region in the page from the start). Never move focus.

  • It's an alert because it belongs on the page; critical ones are dialogs, passing ones toasts
  • Placed next to what it's about, or at the top of the main content
  • Title says the point; errors say how to fix it
  • Doesn't disappear on its own; at most one action
  • Shown later: role="alert" if urgent, role="status" otherwise; focus stays where it was
